# Images and source notes for the Josiah Wedgwood post

These files support the English and Chinese posts
`_posts/2026-11-11-founders-before-vc-josiah-wedgwood.md` and
`_posts/2026-11-11-founders-before-vc-josiah-wedgwood-zh.md`.

```text
cover-en.jpg / cover-zh.jpg   1200x630  cover and social card (title + showroom shelves and tables)
names-en.jpg / names-zh.jpg   1200x675  three names for one ware, after the 1767 letter to Bentley
make_images.py                hand-written SVG -> rsvg-convert (2x) -> Pillow progressive JPEG
```

The images are drawn as SVG by hand, not generated by an image model. The showroom
motif follows Wedgwood's May 1767 letter (services on "two ranges of Tables", vases on
the walls). Regenerate with:

```bash
python3 make_images.py <this-folder> <scratch-folder>
```

Needs `rsvg-convert`, Pillow, and the macOS fonts Georgia, Avenir Next, Songti SC, and PingFang SC.

## Sources

Every claim links to a local reference item. The primary texts are public scans on the
Internet Archive: Eliza Meteyard, *The Life of Josiah Wedgwood* (2 vols, 1865–1866), which
prints Wedgwood's letters; Mrs Delany's correspondence (second series, vol. 1, 1862); and the
1773 and 1774 Wedgwood and Bentley catalogues. Page locators come from the scans' page maps.
Because the sources are themselves archive scans, no separate Wayback snapshots were made.
