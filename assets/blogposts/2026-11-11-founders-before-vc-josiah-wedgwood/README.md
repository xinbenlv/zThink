The illustrations were generated with Codex image_gen on 2026-11-11 and are conceptual, not historical records.
All four images were redrawn in ink-and-wash / engraving style on warm paper and
saved as quality-88 progressive JPEGs. No historical image files occur in these
posts, so no image files were kept unchanged. The obsolete `make_images.py` was removed.

Prompt set: the covers show vases on wall shelves and creamware on two ranges of
tables, with localized titles and `1765–1774`; the diagrams show `Cream-colour`,
`Queen's Ware`, and `Ivory` pointing to one plate, with a localized caption naming
Wedgwood's letter to Bentley, `1767`. The Chinese diagram retains the exact English
names required by its alt text. All prompts require legible text, restrained sepia,
slate blue and terracotta, and no logos, watermarks or signatures.

## Sources

Every claim links to a local reference item. The primary texts are public scans on the
Internet Archive: Eliza Meteyard, *The Life of Josiah Wedgwood* (2 vols, 1865–1866), which
prints Wedgwood's letters; Mrs Delany's correspondence (second series, vol. 1, 1862); and the
1773 and 1774 Wedgwood and Bentley catalogues. Page locators come from the scans' page maps.
Because the sources are themselves archive scans, no separate Wayback snapshots were made.
