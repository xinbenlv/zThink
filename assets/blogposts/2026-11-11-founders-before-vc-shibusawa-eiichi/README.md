# Images for the Shibusawa Eiichi post

Assets for the English post and its Simplified Chinese twin. The illustrations were generated with Codex image_gen on 2026-11-11 and are conceptual, not historical records.

| Image | Dimensions | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cover.png` | 1200 × 630 | English cover and OpenGraph image |
| `cover-zh.png` | 1200 × 630 | Simplified Chinese cover and OpenGraph image |
| `first-national-bank-capital.png` | 1200 × 675 | English capital chart in “The first offering fell short” |
| `first-national-bank-capital-zh.png` | 1200 × 675 | Simplified Chinese capital chart in “第一轮募股没有募满” |

All four PNGs were redrawn in place. No historical documents, photographs, or archival crops were present; no image files were kept unchanged. The four obsolete, unreferenced `src/*.svg` generators were removed.

Prompt set: symbolic blank share certificates for the localized covers; localized stacked capital bars for the charts; consistent fine ink engraving and light wash on warm paper, with muted terracotta, dusty blue, and charcoal. No signatures, logos, or watermarks.

The charts retain planned capital of 3,000,000 yen, Mitsui and Ono’s 2,000,000 yen, public subscriptions of 440,800 yen out of 1,000,000 yen offered, unsold capital of 559,200 yen, and final capital of 2,440,800 yen. The footer retains 71 shareholders, 24,408 shares, and June 11, 1873. Source: the post’s references to *Shibusawa Eiichi denki shiryō*, vol. 4, pp. 22–24 and 33. Hand-drawn bar lengths are approximate; labels give exact amounts.

Both cover crops were recomputed from the certificate subjects. The largest square is 630 × 630; the subject centers put the square at the right edge, so the existing frontmatter values remain `x: 570`, `y: 0`, `size: 630`.
